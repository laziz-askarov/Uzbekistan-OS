# Open decisions

These decisions block later milestones but do not block the repository foundation. Each needs an accountable owner and approval date.

| ID | Decision | Recommended baseline | Owner | Due |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D-001 | Launch workflows | Select 10-15 workflows ranked by user value, risk, and source availability | Product + domain | Week 1 |
| D-002 | Citation metric | Measure claim-level coverage and source validity separately | Product + AI | Week 1 |
| D-003 | Authentication | Guest and email/password first; gate Google OAuth separately | Product + security | Week 1 |
| D-004 | Translation policy | Human review required for published procedural guidance | Content + domain | Week 1 |
| D-005 | Freshness policy | Review interval and expiry behavior by risk level | Content + domain | Week 1 |
| D-006 | Model routing | Configurable generation, embedding, and reranking roles with evaluation gates | AI + platform | Week 2 |
| D-007 | Production hosting | Managed regional services with encrypted backups | Platform + security | Week 2 |
| D-008 | Privacy/retention | Minimize PII; define deletion, analytics consent, and retention before alpha | Product + legal/security | Week 2 |
| D-009 | Dark mode | Tokens now; release only after equivalent accessibility QA | Design + product | Week 2 |
| D-010 | Reliability | Define SLO, RTO, RPO, and error budget | Platform + product | Week 2 |

## Required decision record

For each decision, record context, options considered, chosen option, consequences, owner, approval date, and the artifacts that must change.
